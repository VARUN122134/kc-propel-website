The Propel Method

Our Way of Moving
Things Forward.

A simple five-stage approach designed around one goal: creating meaningful commercial opportunities.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand before we reach out.

    We learn your product, market, ideal customer and sales objectives.

  2. 02

    Map

    Find where the opportunity lives.

    We identify industries, companies, decision-makers and relevant market segments.

  3. 03

    Create

    Build the reason to start a conversation.

    We develop campaigns, messaging and outreach strategies.

  4. 04

    Engage

    Start conversations that make sense.

    We reach the right people through relevant channels and personalized communication.

  5. 05

    Propel

    Move the opportunity forward.

    We qualify, follow up and support the next commercial step.
